## 1. In plain language

Domo is an app for running a home as a group: a shared pantry (what you have,
what ran out, what is in the cart) and a shopping list built from it. You create
a "house" or join an existing one with an invite code, and every member sees and
edits the same list.

For that, Domo needs an account (email, or Google sign-in). **The members of
your house see your name and, if you signed in with Google, your photo.** That
is how they know who is in the house. Your email address is not shown to other
members.

Domo **does not sell data, does not show ads, and does not ask for your tax ID,
address, phone number, GPS location or payment details**. It does not shop for
you: the list is just a note.

The rest of this document explains this in detail, because the law requires a
complete explanation.

## 3. What data is processed

### 3.1 Account data

Created when you sign up and stored by Firebase Authentication:

- **Email address**: your login identifier. Not shown to other house members.
- **Name**: what you type when signing up with email and password, or your
  Google account name. It is the name house members see.
- **Password**: only if you signed up with email and password. Stored
  encrypted by Google and **not readable by Café Labs**.
- **Google account profile photo**: only if you use "Sign in with Google". It
  comes from your Google account.
- **Internal account identifier (UID)**: a code generated by Firebase that
  links you to the houses you belong to.
- **Account creation and last sign-in dates**, and the **IP address** used at
  sign-up and sign-in, recorded by Firebase Authentication for security and
  abuse prevention.
- **Acceptance record**: the version of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you
  accepted and the date and time. It is kept in a private record of yours in
  Cloud Firestore that only you can access, to prove the acceptance happened.
- **List of houses you are waiting to join**: a private record, also only yours,
  with the codes of houses you asked to join and were not yet approved for. It
  exists so account deletion can clear those requests.

### 3.2 House and member data (visible to other members)

When you create or join a house, Domo stores in Cloud Firestore:

- **About the house**: the name its creator gave it, the 6-character invite
  code, who created it and when, and the chosen order of pantry categories.
- **About each member, including you**:
  - **display name**: the name you typed when signing up with email and
    password, or your Google account name ("Usuário" if the account has no
    name);
  - **profile photo link** from your Google account, if any;
  - **role** ("Administrator" or "Member", a label only);
  - **status**: active or pending (awaiting approval).

**Who sees this data:** the **active** members of the house. Someone who enters
the invite code and asks to join is **pending** and **cannot see the member list
or the items** until approved; they only see the house name, which the code
reveals. Still, share the code only with people you know.

For invite codes to work, Domo also stores a record linking the code to the
house, containing the **house name**. Anyone with a Domo account who enters a
valid code can see that house's name.

### 3.3 Pantry items and shopping list

Typed by house members and visible to all **active** members (pending members
cannot see them):

- item name and category;
- status (have, out, in cart);
- if quantity tracking is on: quantity and minimum stock;
- date of the last status change and **which member made it** (by UID).

**Free-text fields.** The house name and item names accept any text and are
visible to the other members. Do not write personal information there (names of
people, addresses, health data such as medicine names, etc.) beyond what the
house needs to get organized.

### 3.4 Technical data

- **Login session**: kept on your device (in the web app, in the browser's
  local storage) so you don't have to sign in every time. Not an advertising
  cookie.
- **Profile photo**: loaded directly from Google's servers and cached on your
  device.
- **Google technical logs**: when you use the app, your IP address and basic
  request data reach Google Firebase's infrastructure, which logs them for
  operation and security.
- **Fonts**: when the app opens, fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts, which
  sends your IP address to Google.

### 3.6 Usage data (app measurement)

Domo uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand, in aggregate,
whether people manage to use the app and come back to it. It collects:

- **App instance identifier**: a Firebase-generated code tied to the app
  installation on that device or browser, not to your identity.
- **Approximate location**: country and, at most, region/state, derived from
  the IP address. **Not** GPS.
- **Device and version**: model, operating system, Domo version, language.
- **Automatic usage**: screens opened, sessions, first open.
- **Six Domo events**, recording only that the action happened, with at most a
  count or a status: house created; request to join a house; item status change
  (from which status to which); cart closed (number of items); item quantity
  updated (resulting status); category order changed (number of categories).

**Not** included: your name, email, UID, house name, invite code, item or
category names.

- **Advertising ID**: the Android app does **not** collect the advertising
  identifier (the permission was removed and collection turned off in
  Analytics), and "Google signals" personalization is also off.
- **In the web app**, measurement uses Google Analytics cookies and browser
  local storage. There is no prior-blocking banner: measurement relies on
  legitimate interest (section 5) and you can turn it off at any time under
  Profile → Privacy → Usage measurement.
- This data is **not** used for advertising, not combined with data from other
  apps or sites, and not sold. `TODO: confirmar` in the Google Analytics

- You can turn this measurement off in the app, under Profile → Privacy →

  the app or browser data, measurement returns to the default (on) and you need

### 3.7 What is NOT collected

Domo does **not** collect or ask for: identity documents; phone number or

Apart from Google Analytics for Firebase (section 3.6), the app contains **no

## 8. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you may at any time: **confirm** whether we hold your
data; **access** it (everything Domo stores about you is shown in the app; you
can also request a copy by email); **correct** it (items in the app; name and
photo in your Google account — `TODO: confirmar` the display name stays as typed
at sign-up and the app has no screen to change it yet; ask by email); **request deletion**; **request portability** (Domo
has no in-app export yet; we send a copy in an open, readable format (JSON) on
request); **know who** your data is shared with (section 2); **withdraw** any
separately given consent; **object** to processing, including turning off usage
measurement in the app (section 3.6); and **complain** to the ANPD.

**How:** what the app lets you do yourself (delete your account, turn off usage
measurement) you do in the app; for the rest, write to `privacidade@cafelabs.net`.
We reply within **15 days**.

### Account deletion in practice

1. Request deletion in the app (Profile → Delete account), via the public page
   `https://domo.cafelabs.net/excluir-conta`, or by email to
   `privacidade@cafelabs.net`. In the app, for security, Domo asks you to confirm
   your password (or sign in again with Google) before deleting.
2. Deleted: your login account (Firebase Authentication), your member data (name
   and photo) in every house you belong to, your pending join requests and the
   private records in section 3.1.
3. **Houses you created: the house and all its items are deleted for every
   member.** The app warns you before you confirm. Transferring a house to
   another member does not exist yet; if you do not want the house to end, do not
   delete your account before talking to the other members.
4. **Houses you joined:** you leave them; items you added stay, as they are the
   house's shared list, and the only trace is an internal code that does not
   identify you.
5. Backups expire within the period in section 7 and are not restored.

Deletion is **permanent and irreversible**.

- **13 to 17:** any processing is done in the adolescent's best interest (LGPD

parties. Domo shows no ads.

## 10. Security

- All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
- Data at rest is encrypted by Google Firebase infrastructure.
- Access is enforced server-side by Firestore Security Rules: only members of a
  house read its data, only active members read and edit items, and only the
  house creator approves, removes members or deletes the house. These rules
  are tested automatically on every change.
- Passwords are stored and verified by Google, encrypted, and are not readable
  by Café Labs.
- Administrative access to the infrastructure is individual and restricted.

No system is 100% secure. If a security incident may create relevant risk or
harm to you, Café Labs will notify the **ANPD** and **you** under LGPD art. 48,
within the deadline set by ANPD regulation (currently **3 business days** from
becoming aware of the incident, Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024), stating what
happened, which data was affected, the risks and what is being done.

**Protect your password and your house code.** Café Labs will never ask for your
password by email, message or phone.
