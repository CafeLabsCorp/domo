## 1. What Domo is

Domo is a **shared household organization** app: a shared pantry, organized by
category and status (have, out, in cart), and a shopping list built from it.
Items are organized into **houses**, and each house has members who see and
edit the same list.

Domo is provided by **Café Labs**, operated by Felipe Portes Antunes. Café Labs
does not yet have its own company registration number (CNPJ).
Contact: `contato@cafelabs.net`.

### 1.1 What Domo is NOT

- **Not a store or delivery service.** Domo sells, buys, charges and delivers
  nothing. "In cart" is just a note for the house.
- **No guarantee of what is in your pantry.** The app shows what members
  wrote down. If the note is wrong, the cupboard is right.
- **No reminders or alerts** for low stock or expiry dates.
- **Not a messaging channel** between members, beyond the list itself.

## 3. Houses, members and invites

- **Whoever creates a house is responsible for it.** Only that person can
  approve or refuse join requests, remove members, change members' roles,
  rename the house and **delete the house**.
- **Roles ("Administrator", "Member") are labels only** and grant no extra
  powers.
- **Deleting a house deletes all its items for every member**, permanently.
- **Every active member** can add, edit and delete items, change their status,
  close the cart and reorder the house's categories.
- **The invite code grants access to the house.** Anyone with a Domo account
  who has the code can ask to join. While awaiting approval the person is
  pending and sees only the house name; **they see no members and no items**.
  Share the code only with people you trust. The house creator is
  responsible for whom they approve.
- By joining a house you agree that your name and photo (from your Google
  account, if any) are visible to the other active members. Your email address
  is not shown (Privacy Policy, item 3.2).
